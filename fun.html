01
macOS · Windows · open source

AI Stack

I didn't want to pay for Grammarly and Granola on top of Claude, so I rebuilt both, running on the Claude plan I already have.

  • Writing assistant: red underlines for mistakes and blue for clumsy phrasing, in every app on macOS and every text box in Chrome. Hover for the fix, or press Ctrl+Alt+A to accept everything.
  • Meeting notes: it notices when a call starts, transcribes both sides on-device, and has Claude write structured notes with action items when you hang up.

Native Swift app on macOS; a Chrome extension plus a small .NET helper on Windows. Audio never leaves the computer.

02
Autonomous agents · Kaggle

AI for data science

A turnkey template for an autonomous multi-agent data-science team. You launch the orchestrator once and it loops forever: it reads the brief, reviews finished experiments, commits improvements, decides what to try next, and spawns specialist sub-agents in parallel. Each sub-agent is a one-shot claude -p process that owns one file and one task.

Spaceship Titanic (accuracy)0.7973 → 0.8071577 / 2209

One-hour runs, no human guidance. Along the way it found and fixed a label-encoding bug, built a LightGBM + CatBoost + XGBoost blend, and engineered spatial and redshift features.

03
Fully automated media

Have you seen my quant

An experiment in a fully automated AI product: short, animated explainers of classic quant and probability interview puzzles, with no human in the loop. Every night a pipeline:

  • writes new puzzle scripts
  • animates every scene
  • runs Monte-Carlo proofs
  • narrates & captions
  • quality-gates the video
  • posts it
  • reads the views back

The view counts feed back into what it makes next. The puzzles also live on the road, a free, Duolingo-style path through probability and maths where every question is drawn rather than written.
